We want you to love your Rano for years. If a piece is not right for you, you can return it within 14 days of delivery. Here is how it works.

Your 14-day right of withdrawal

As a consumer in the European Union you may cancel your purchase within 14 days after you, or someone you appoint, received the product, without giving a reason. For orders with several items delivered separately, the 14 days start when you receive the last item. This right also applies to sale and Black Friday items.

How to return

  1. Email us at ranoeurope@gmail.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and the item(s) you want to return. You may also use the model withdrawal form below, but you do not have to.
  2. We reply with the return address and instructions.
  3. Send the item back within 14 days after telling us you want to return it.

Condition of returned items

You may handle the product as you would in a shop to check it. Please return it unused, clean, complete and in its original packaging, including the dust bag, tags and any gift box. Leather marks easily: if an item shows signs of use beyond what is needed to inspect it, we may deduct the reduced value from your refund.

Return shipping costs

Return shipping is at your own cost, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong product. We recommend a tracked shipping method and keeping your proof of postage, as the return is your responsibility until it reaches us.

Refunds

We refund the full purchase price, including the original standard shipping costs, within 14 days of receiving your cancellation. We may wait with the refund until we have received the item back or you have shown proof of return, whichever comes first. The refund is made with the same payment method you used, unless agreed otherwise.

Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. Simply return the item and place a new order for the colour or piece you prefer, so you are sure of availability.

Faulty or damaged items

Every piece is checked before it leaves our studio. If something is wrong, email us within a reasonable time after you discover it with your order number and photos. Under EU consumer law you have a legal guarantee: products must conform to the agreement, and faults that were present at delivery are repaired, replaced or refunded free of charge. Normal wear, scratches, colour changes from sunlight or water, and damage from incorrect use or care are not covered.

Exceptions

Personalised items made to your specification, such as engraved or custom corporate gifting orders, cannot be returned unless faulty. Gift cards are non-refundable.
