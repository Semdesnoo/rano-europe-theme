Every Rano piece leaves our studio carefully packed in its dust bag and travels with DHL, fully tracked, to your door. Shipping is always free.

Shipping costs

Shipping is free on every order, to every country we deliver to. There are no minimum order amounts.

Where we ship

We ship to the countries you can select at checkout, including the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Austria and Ireland. For orders outside the European Union, import duties and local taxes may apply. These are set by the destination country and are paid by the recipient on delivery.

Processing time

Orders are packed and handed to DHL within 1-2 working days (Monday to Friday, excluding Dutch public holidays). During launches and sale periods such as Black Friday this can take up to 3 working days.

Tracking your order

As soon as your order is on its way you receive an email with your DHL tracking number. You can follow your parcel on our Track my order page or directly on dhl.com.

Missed delivery

If you are not home, DHL will usually try again the next working day or deliver your parcel to a nearby DHL ServicePoint. You can change the delivery day or location via your DHL tracking link.

Wrong address

Please check your shipping address carefully at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us at ranoeurope@gmail.com as soon as possible. We can only change the address before your order has been handed to DHL.

Lost or damaged parcels

The risk of loss and damage passes to you once the parcel has been delivered. If your parcel is lost in transit or arrives damaged, contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date with your order number and, for damage, photos of the packaging and product. We will open an investigation with DHL and send a replacement or refund once it is confirmed.
