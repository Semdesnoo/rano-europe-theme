Who we share it with

We only share data with parties that help us run our store, under data processing agreements: Shopify (our store platform), our payment providers, DHL (delivery), our email marketing platform and, with your consent, analytics and advertising partners such as Meta, TikTok and Google. Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers, including Shopify, may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case we rely on an adequacy decision or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses to protect your data.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice data are kept for 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law. Customer service messages are kept for up to 2 years after our last contact. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Applications that do not lead to a collaboration are deleted after 12 months.

Cookies

We use necessary cookies to make the store work. Statistics, marketing and preference cookies are only placed after you agree in our cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your cookies or using the cookie settings.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. Email ranoeurope@gmail.com and we respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. Our store runs on a secured (HTTPS) connection and payments are handled by certified payment providers.
